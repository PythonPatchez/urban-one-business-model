Proposed Business Model

The proposed model combines advertiser qualification, campaign intake, consent-aware data validation, audience segmentation, eligible inventory matching, campaign delivery, performance measurement, and continuous optimization.

Business value

  • More consistent advertiser proposals and campaign workflows.
  • Better visibility into campaign delivery, conversion, renewal, and revenue metrics.
  • Governed use of audience signals and fallback to contextual targeting when personal data are unavailable or not permitted.
  • Clearer links between sales assumptions and measured outcomes.

Required capabilities

CRM and campaign records, data-governance rules, inventory availability, campaign analytics, reliable attribution definitions, and a Power BI reporting layer. The model remains conceptual until calibrated against historical observations.
