Introduction

This website documents a proposed AI-assisted advertising process for Urban One, Inc. The project examines process flow, sales strategy, quantitative sales scenarios, domestic advertising price-demand feedback, and an internet-oriented stock-and-flow model.

Evidence rule: Urban One's FY2025 Form 10-K reports consolidated net revenue of $374.371 million, compared with $449.674 million in FY2024. This public company figure is separate from the illustrative campaign scenario used in the sales model. Read the 2025 Form 10-K.

Project objectives

  • Model an advertising workflow with explicit data governance and exception handling.
  • Evaluate an illustrative sales funnel with reproducible formulas and uncertainty analysis.
  • Represent pricing feedback and internet-advertiser acquisition/retention dynamics.
  • Translate scenario outputs into Power BI reporting recommendations.
